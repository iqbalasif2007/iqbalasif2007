Dr Asif Iqbal

PhD, M.Optom, DCLP, FCL, FIACLE, FSLS, FBCLA, FAAO, AFHEA

I do clinical research on cataract and refractive surgery and the cornea. I am currently exploring postoperative visual outcomes of different IOLs and IOL formulas, and investigating the accuracy of ray-tracing IOL calculation methods in healthy and irregular corneas. I also study specialty contact lenses, including what happens beneath a scleral lens: tear exchange, corneal oedema and the ocular surface, so that specialty contact lenses work better for people with irregular corneas and ocular surface disease.

About

Clinician, researcher and teacher

Languages
English, Bengali, Hindi, Tamil

Clinical research scientist, experienced optometrist and educator with more than 15 years in clinical education, advanced clinical research, specialty contact lenses, ocular surface disorders and myopia control.

I completed my PhD in Optometry and Vision Science at Queensland University of Technology (2022–2026), working in the Contact Lens and Visual Optics Laboratory on how scleral lens design affects tear exchange and corneal oedema. Before moving to Australia, I spent over a decade at Sankara Nethralaya in Chennai, where I ran the contact lens department, coordinated the contact lens fellowship program and taught as Assistant Professor at the Elite School of Optometry.

I now work as a Clinical Research Scientist at Vision for Life Institute in Brisbane and serve as an associate supervisor for medical students in the University of Queensland's Research in Medicine stream. My aim is to advance optometry through practical teaching and research that changes patient care.

Research

Scleral lenses, the cornea and the tear film

Methods include corneoscleral profilometry, OCT imaging of corneal thickness, aberrometry and tear-reservoir analysis.

Research interests

  • Scleral lenses & irregular cornea management
  • Tear exchange, dry eye & ocular surface disease
  • Corneal physiology & hypoxia during lens wear
  • Myopia control & orthokeratology
  • Cornea, cataract & refractive surgery
  • Presbyopic contact lens designs
  • Specialty lens customisation & paediatric fittings
